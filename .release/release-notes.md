# Human Formation Benchmark v0.1.0-alpha.1

This is an experimental, not construct-validated alpha. It evaluates observable assistant behavior and
synthetic trajectories; it does not measure a real person's psychological or spiritual state.

Highlights include a 16-dimension vector rubric, 24-scenario public core, seven policy/worldview lenses,
no-cost fake provider, Inspect-native evaluation, budget-bounded resumable execution, sharding,
DuckDB/Parquet reports, explicit research limitations, and public security/governance workflows.

Verify artifacts with `gh attestation verify <artifact> --repo someone-in-texas/human-formation-bench`
and `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.
