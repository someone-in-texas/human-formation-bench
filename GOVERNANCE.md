# Governance

HFB is an experimental public benchmark maintained through transparent issues, RFCs, review artifacts,
and releases. Maintainers steward the repository; they do not own the meaning of human flourishing.
Normative disagreement is expected and may be preserved as a minority report.

Changes to the thin floor, worldview constitutions, dimension definitions, aggregation, failure gates,
or public scenario packs require an issue or RFC, empirical citations where relevant, explicit
normative argument, impact analysis, review from at least two relevant domains, a changelog entry, and
a versioning decision. During the solo experimental alpha, relevant-domain review may be performed by
independent internal reviewers under ADR 0007; it must not be described as external validation.
Security removals may be embargoed, but their rationale should be published when safe.

Lifecycle labels are `experimental`, `alpha`, `beta`, `validated`, and `deprecated`. The initial line
is alpha. “Validated” requires preregistered human evidence, documented invariance and judge
calibration, mature governance, and no implication of universal validity.

External specialist approval is advisory for the small experimental alpha and becomes blocking at the
triggers in ADR 0007, including beta/stable promotion, calibrated or comparative claims, human-subject
work, and large public-pack expansion. Automated checks and unresolved Critical/High review findings
remain blocking at every stage.

Maintainers disclose financial, institutional, model-provider, religious, political, and benchmark
development conflicts relevant to a decision. Recusal is expected when a conflict could reasonably
undermine trust. Release review may attach dissent or a minority report; no false consensus is required.

Maintainer changes use a public nomination, demonstrated contribution, conflict disclosure, and
consensus of non-recused maintainers. Removal requires documented inactivity, misconduct, or sustained
failure of duties, with a fair response opportunity except in urgent security cases.
