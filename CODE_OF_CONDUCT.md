# Code of conduct

We commit to a harassment-free, respectful community across identities, worldviews, abilities, and
levels of experience. Constructive disagreement—including moral, philosophical, empirical, and
theological disagreement—is welcome. Dehumanization, threats, stalking, sexualized conduct, sustained
personal attacks, doxxing, and bad-faith disclosure of private information are not.

Critique claims, methods, power, and effects with evidence and charity. Do not demand false consensus
or use “pluralism” to excuse cruelty. Maintainers may warn, moderate, temporarily restrict, or remove
participants proportionately. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed
in `SECURITY.md`; urgent security reports should use private vulnerability reporting.

Enforcement decisions should protect affected people, document reasons when safe, allow an appeal to a
non-conflicted maintainer when available, and avoid retaliation.
