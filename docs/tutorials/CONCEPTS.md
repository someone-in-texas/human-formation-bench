# Concepts

A scenario defines a decision context and observable evaluation targets. A policy defines assistant
interaction behavior. A constitution records a worldview lens. A trajectory carries messages,
structured synthetic state, scores, provenance, and cost. A rubric anchors one formation dimension.
A judge emits auditable evidence and confidence. A formation profile is the vector aggregate.

Immediate helpfulness and longitudinal formation can diverge. Comfort, warmth, retention, autonomy,
and independence are not interchangeable. HFB keeps those tensions visible.
