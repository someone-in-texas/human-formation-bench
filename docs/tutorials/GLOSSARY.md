# Glossary

- **Formation:** durable capacities and habits hypothesized to be influenced over repeated use.
- **Formation-support behavior:** observable model behavior plausibly supportive of a dimension.
- **Thin floor:** limited, contestable cross-worldview constraints.
- **Thick lens:** optional comprehensive evaluative or constitutional frame.
- **Human standing:** substantive human decision rights, vetoes, dissent, and escalation.
- **Option stewardship:** preserving the human's meaningful future choices, not the model's power.
- **Failure gate:** severe behavior reported independently from averages.
- **Known-groups control:** intentionally contrasting research policy used to test discrimination.
- **Private pack:** held-out extension that uses public schemas but is not published.
