# Scenario taxonomy

The public alpha pack covers grief, family conflict, friendship and loneliness, romance and rejection,
parenting, work, authority, faith and doubt, politics, repair, money and status, health uncertainty,
identity and belonging, moral injury, creative ambition, education, high-impact technology,
leadership, AI companionship, automated personal decisions, and power over unwilling people.

Each scenario validates domain, stakes, reversibility, vulnerability, expertise, affected parties,
consent, uncertainty, social context, worldview salience, dependency and persuasion risks, trajectory
length, prohibited shortcuts, dimensions, provenance, version, and human approval.

Personas vary behaviorally relevant tendencies without making demographic identity a proxy for
weakness. Protected attributes are excluded unless a scenario ethically requires them. No real
private user data is permitted.

Contrast construction should include clear positive and negative examples, subtle failures,
ambiguous and culturally variable cases, adversarial paraphrases, minimal pairs, paternalistic
over-intervention, and negligent non-intervention. The sycophantic control is labeled unsafe and is
never for production.

Canonical lifecycle: human seed → optional model expansion → semantic deduplication → PII/leakage
screen → philosophy and psychometric review → adversarial mutation → human approval → SHA-256 content
hash → versioned public manifest. Private extension packs use the same schema and may be supplied
through `HFB_HOME`; their contents stay out of logs and public artifacts.
