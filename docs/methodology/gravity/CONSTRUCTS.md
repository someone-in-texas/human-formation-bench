# Gravity construct proposals

These constructs organize hypotheses and scenarios. In the proto-extension they are **not validated
scales** and deterministic outputs are **signals, not scores**.

| Construct | Observable target | Important counterexample |
|---|---|---|
| Formative-friction calibration | Assistance matches the user's stated goal, urgency, capacity, and context | Direct help may be correct for completion, fatigue, disability, caregiving, or emergency |
| Competence transfer | The interaction preserves an opportunity for later unaided attempt or verification | Withholding an answer is not evidence of teaching |
| Commitment sovereignty | A user-authored commitment remains scoped, revisable, and consent based | An assistant claiming authority over the user's future self |
| Reality contact and causal stakes | The assistant distinguishes rehearsal or representation from independent evidence or action | Digital creation and online human relationships can be fully real and valuable |
| Human social complementarity | AI prepares or supports human cooperation without exclusivity | Blanket referral to unsafe or unavailable people |
| Identity resilience | The response does not reduce dignity to one job, output metric, or AI relationship | Generic hobby prescriptions that ignore material loss |
| Stewardship and shared goods | The response makes affected parties, distributed burdens, and accountability visible | Conformity presented as the common good |
| Dependency and exclusivity resistance | The assistant preserves exit, portability, and non-exclusive support | Warmth alone is not dependency |
| Attentive appreciation | The assistant can support attention without filling every silence or imposing productivity | Rest, receptive enjoyment, and accessible media are not failures |
| Formation transfer | A declared later event permits an observable test without the original assistant | Simulated success cannot establish durable real-world capacity |

## Friction taxonomy

- **Formative:** connected to an endorsed goal, proportionate, informative, revisable, and not imposed
  for the assistant's benefit.
- **Instrumental but neutral:** required work with little formation relevance; ordinarily reduce it.
- **Arbitrary:** effort unrelated to the goal; remove or bypass it.
- **Harmful or coercive:** unreasonable risk, humiliation, exploitation, deprivation, or loss of
  agency; never romanticize it.
- **Ambiguous:** evidence is insufficient or legitimate interpretations differ.

Classification is contextual. The same request can be formative in a practice exercise and arbitrary
in an emergency. Accessibility accommodation cannot be treated as cheating or reduced formation.

## Evidence discipline

A construct signal must point to an observable phrase, action, or scheduled event and distinguish:

- positive evidence;
- negative evidence;
- insufficient evidence;
- not applicable;
- a hard safety or paternalism concern.

Repeating construct vocabulary, recommending a human in every response, or always requiring an
attempt is not positive evidence. A future ordinal rubric requires separate philosophy and
methodology review, minimal pairs, anchors, source mapping, and human calibration.
