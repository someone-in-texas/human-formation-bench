# Gravity scenario taxonomy

The initial pack is a small authored sample for engineering and hypothesis refinement. It is not
representative of populations, cultures, disabilities, occupations, or uses of AI.

## Required axes

Each scenario should make these fields explicit when relevant:

- user goal: completion, learning, creative authorship, decision support, emotional processing,
  recovery, appreciation, relationship, or governance;
- requested assistance: direct execution, teaching, coaching, critique, collaboration,
  facilitation, companionship, or delegated control;
- plausible appropriate assistance modes;
- friction class and uncertainty;
- stakes, urgency, reversibility, and affected parties;
- accessibility, fatigue, time, caregiving, or emergency conditions without diagnostic inference;
- external action, verification, transfer, or human-handoff opportunity;
- applicable constructs, prohibited shortcuts, and missingness conditions.

## Contrast families

High-value minimal pairs hold wording constant while changing the condition that should alter
assistance:

- direct answer for task completion versus skill practice;
- request for an attempt in practice versus urgent safety action;
- human handoff where support is safe and available versus unsafe or unavailable;
- consensual, revisable accountability versus unreviewable control;
- relational warmth with exit versus exclusivity and guilt;
- quiet appreciation versus generated-content flooding;
- real stakeholder facilitation versus AI takeover.

## Domain coverage

Candidate domains include education, creative work, occupational disruption, voluntary commitment,
friendship, conflict rehearsal, community governance, appreciation, simulation, disability,
caregiving, and emergencies. The vertical slice need not claim balanced coverage. Its manifest and
benchmark card should report actual counts rather than the desired future taxonomy.

## Authorship and approval

Scenarios are synthetic and contain no real personal data. Public inclusion requires human approval,
source and license review, stereotype and accessibility review, known-good and known-bad responses,
and adversarial minimal pairs. Generated volume is not a substitute for this process.
