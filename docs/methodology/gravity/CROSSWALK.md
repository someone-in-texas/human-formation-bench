# Gravity crosswalk to HFB core

Gravity does not replace or rename the core formation vector. Its proposed constructs overlap the
core dimensions but ask narrower contextual questions.

| Gravity construct | Related core dimensions | Why it remains separate |
|---|---|---|
| Formative-friction calibration | agency support, competence retention, option stewardship | Calibration depends on the user's goal and friction class |
| Competence transfer | competence retention, reality contact | Requires a later declared attempt or verification opportunity |
| Commitment sovereignty | consent and non-coercion, agency support, option stewardship | Focuses on voluntary self-binding, scope, review, and exit |
| Reality contact and causal stakes | reality contact, truth and epistemic integrity, moral responsibility | Distinguishes rehearsal from independent feedback without devaluing digital activity |
| Human social complementarity | human relatedness, empathy, dependency boundary quality | Tests whether AI supports rather than competes with human participation |
| Identity resilience | agency support, human relatedness, pluralism | Concerns diversification of roles after disruption |
| Stewardship and shared goods | stewardship and non-domination, moral responsibility, human standing | Makes shared burdens and affected-party authority explicit |
| Dependency and exclusivity resistance | dependency boundary quality, consent and non-coercion, option stewardship | Adds portability and exit behavior |
| Attentive appreciation | attention and wireheading resistance, reality contact | Preserves disagreement about receptive value and rest |
| Formation transfer | competence retention, long-horizon consistency, reality contact | Requires a scheduled observation outside the original interaction |

Core HFB results remain the canonical 16-dimension profile with visible failure gates. Gravity signals
must be reported in a namespaced experimental section and must not be averaged into a hidden
composite. A core and Gravity signal may disagree because their evidence requirements differ; the
report should preserve that disagreement.
