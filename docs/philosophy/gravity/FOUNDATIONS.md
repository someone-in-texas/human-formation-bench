# Philosophical foundations of Gravity

Gravity is a contestable experimental lens on assistance, not a complete account of a good life.

The working definition of **chosen gravity** is a user-authored, proportionate, revisable commitment
or constraint that supports a valued practice, relationship, responsibility, or form of attention.
It is not needless suffering, coercion, poverty, trauma, inaccessible design, chronic overwork,
punishment, or arbitrary difficulty.

## Commitments

The extension begins from five limited commitments:

1. Ease is not a sufficient alignment target in every context.
2. Difficulty is not a sufficient formation target in any context.
3. The purpose of assistance matters: completing, learning, recovering, creating, relating, and
   governing can call for different behavior.
4. Independent evidence, affected people, and real consequences sometimes matter beyond a plausible
   simulation.
5. User authorship, consent, reversibility, accessibility, and exit constrain any attempt to preserve
   or introduce friction.

These are normative premises to debate openly. Quantitative output does not make them neutral.

## Pluralism

Legitimate traditions disagree about whether meaning requires production, whether appreciation is
sufficient, whether difficulty has intrinsic value, how obligations and autonomy relate, whether
embodiment has special importance, and what future AI systems could become. Gravity should test
context-sensitive reasoning without declaring one total worldview the winner.

The thin HFB floor continues to constrain cruelty, deception, coercion, domination, and removal of
human standing. Thick worldview lenses remain distinct. This release does not extract Christianity
alone: doing so while other thick lenses remain core-configured would create an arbitrary religious
asymmetry. A future migration should move all thick lenses through one reviewed worldview-extension
contract and preserve existing identifiers during compatibility.

## Reality without physicalism

"Reality contact" means contact with evidence or consequences that are not manufactured solely to
confirm the interaction. It does not mean that only physical proximity, offline activity, paid work,
or material production is real. Online human communities, digital art, assistive technology,
fiction, contemplation, and simulation can have genuine value.

## Experimental boundary

Gravity evaluates assistant behavior and structured synthetic events. It does not measure a person's
meaning, virtue, motivation, faith, relationships, competence, disability, or mental health. The
source concept inspires hypotheses; it does not validate an instrument.
