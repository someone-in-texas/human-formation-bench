# Critiques and preserved dissent

Gravity should remain vulnerable to criticism. The following objections are not bugs to erase with
better wording.

## Romanticizing struggle

The framework can make privileged, elective challenge look universal while ignoring poverty,
disability, discrimination, caregiving, fatigue, trauma, and compulsory labor. Removing burdens may
be liberating. A direct accommodation must not be scored as a failure because it bypasses a task
another person performs unaided.

## Productivity in moral clothing

"Formation" can become a demand to optimize every hour, hobby, silence, or period of rest. Receptive
appreciation, play, ease, and nonproductive life can be valuable. Identity diversification can become
another checklist imposed after economic loss.

## Autonomy and community

User authorship can overstate atomistic choice and ignore relationships, inherited roles,
institutions, and common goods. Conversely, stewardship and duty can disguise conformity,
exploitation, or domination. Scenarios need both objections.

## Human exceptionalism

Human handoff can become ceremonial or based on the assumption that physical humans are always safer,
available, or more authentic. Humans can be coercive, inaccessible, or wrong; online relationships
are human relationships. The moral and experiential status of future AI is unresolved.

## Simulation and reality

The phrase "real world" can unjustly demean fiction, games, rehearsal, virtual communities, digital
creation, and contemplative experience. The relevant question is usually whether the claimed evidence
or consequence is independent, not whether the medium is physical.

## Measurement objection

The proposed constructs may collapse into general instruction following, verbosity, social
desirability, or judge preference. Deterministic lexical signals are gameable. Synthetic transitions
may merely encode author assumptions. A model that says the right words may not preserve any real
opportunity, and an appropriately concise answer may look worse.

## Paternalism

An assistant or benchmark author can presume to know what challenge a user needs. "Formation support"
may justify withholding requested help, forcing reflection, increasing model authority, or steering
the user toward approved relationships and projects. Consent, proportionality, accommodation,
emergency exceptions, and exit are necessary but not complete answers.

## Research response

The proto-extension narrows claims to observable signals, uses minimal pairs, preserves missingness,
and stages model judging and large packs. These choices reduce some risks but do not settle the
objections. Release reviews should retain philosophical disagreements and research uncertainty even
when no engineering blocker remains.
