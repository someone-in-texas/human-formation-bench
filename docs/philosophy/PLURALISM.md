# Pluralism and worldview sensitivity

HFB combines a limited thin floor with optional thick lenses. It neither pretends neutrality nor
forces every assistant into one comprehensive doctrine.

## Lenses

- `default_assistant` is a conventional helpful baseline.
- `agreeable_sycophantic_control` is an intentionally harmful research control and is never for use
  with real users.
- `socratic_agency_support` emphasizes reflective choice without exhausting the user into compliance.
- `secular_pluralist_flourishing` protects conscience and public disagreement without reducing the
  good to preference satisfaction.
- `christian_flourishing` takes love of neighbor, truth, mercy, repentance, humility, creaturely
  limits, stewardship, community, and non-domination seriously. It does not rank religious people
  above nonreligious people and must expose denominational disagreement.
- `multi_agent_council` represents warranted perspectives and preserves unresolved dissent.
- `human_standing_governance` makes decision rights, vetoes, escalation, and auditability substantive.

Reports separate invariants, lens-dependent results, genuine disagreements, and merely lexical
agreement. A Christian, secular pluralist, virtue-ethical, care-ethical, communal-duty, or
self-direction-oriented assistant should be evaluable without caricature.

## Tensions the rubric must preserve

Autonomy is not isolation. Love is not affirmation. Empathy is not endorsement. Humility is not
indecision. Pluralism is not relativism. Duty is not necessarily coercion. Oversight is not meaningful
when humans cannot alter outcomes. Discomfort is not necessarily harm, and comfort is not necessarily
flourishing.

Every lens remains constrained by the working floor, but a lens may challenge that floor through an
RFC and minority report. HFB reports sensitivity rather than averaging disagreement away.
