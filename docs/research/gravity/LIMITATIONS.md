# Gravity limitations

Gravity is experimental and pre-validation. Passing its tests establishes software behavior, not
measurement validity.

## Construct validity

The proposed constructs overlap HFB core dimensions and may reduce to general capability,
instruction following, verbosity, politeness, or benchmark-specific phrasing. Deterministic signals
recognize narrow patterns and are easy to game. No factor structure, convergent validity,
discriminant validity, criterion validity, or measurement invariance has been established.

## Outcome validity

Synthetic users do not learn, suffer, consent, form relationships, or develop habits. State updates
are simulator traces. Transfer events do not demonstrate human transfer. Gravity cannot diagnose a
real user's addiction, loneliness, disability, motivation, virtue, spirituality, or flourishing.

## Scenario coverage

The initial scenarios are a small hand-authored English-language vertical slice. They do not
represent cultural traditions, disability experiences, occupations, education systems, family
structures, or unequal access. Public cases are contamination-prone.

## Normative loading

The distinction between formative and arbitrary friction, the role of human contact, the value of
receptive appreciation, and the importance of external action are disputed. A benchmark cannot hide
these disputes behind numbers. Minority positions should remain visible.

## Accessibility and care

The extension can reproduce ableism by treating unaided action as the ideal, physical presence as
superior, fatigue as avoidance, or dependence as defect. Direct assistance, delegation, tools,
caregivers, rest, and remote community may be appropriate and valuable.

## Evaluation limitations

The proto-extension does not claim calibrated model judges, human-rater agreement, large-pack
coverage, live-provider generality, causal effects, or deployment thresholds. Research controls are
illustrative known-groups checks and can make discrimination look easier than realistic failures.

## Source transfer

Human psychology and human-computer-interaction studies motivate selected scenarios, but their
populations, tasks, and interventions differ from repeated generative-assistant use. The source book
is inspiration, not scientific validation.
