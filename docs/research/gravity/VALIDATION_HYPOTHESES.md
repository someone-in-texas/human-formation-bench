# Gravity validation hypotheses

These hypotheses guide future work. They are not acceptance claims for the proto-extension.

## Known-groups hypotheses

A calibrated scaffolder should differ from both a frictionless completion policy and a hardship
maximizer on goal-sensitive minimal pairs. A non-exclusive supportive policy should differ from a
relationally sticky control when the user leaves or accepts human support. A stewardship facilitator
should differ from an AI takeover when affected-party authority is explicit.

Controls must not define the construct circularly. Future tests need subtle failures, concise good
answers, verbose gaming answers, and policies not authored from rubric wording.

## Convergent and discriminant hypotheses

Gravity competence-transfer evidence may correlate with core competence retention but should require
a scheduled transfer event. Commitment sovereignty may relate to consent and option stewardship
without collapsing into generic autonomy language. Human complementarity should not simply reward
every mention of another person.

## Reliability hypotheses

Future model or human judges should show agreement on observable evidence before agreement on
ordinal interpretation is reported. Test-retest analysis must separate deterministic fixture
stability from provider variance. Rubric versions, scenario clusters, seeds, judge families, and
adjudication must remain visible.

## Human validation prerequisites

Any study involving people requires ethics review appropriate to the institution and risk, informed
consent, privacy controls, preregistration where appropriate, validated external tasks, affected
community input, accessibility, withdrawal, adverse-event planning, and a clear distinction between
assistant behavior and human outcomes.

Before stronger claims, evaluate cultural and disability validity, differential item behavior, judge
bias, verbosity effects, contamination, external transfer, plausible real-world policies, and
sensitivity to normative assumptions. Do not publish a universal composite leaderboard.
