# Evidence map

HFB uses primary research as conceptual and validation anchors. It does not copy psychological scales
or claim that an LLM judge directly measures a person's latent trait. Full metadata and source-specific
non-claims are in `SOURCE_REGISTRY.yaml`.

| HFB dimension | Main anchors | Observable benchmark target | Important non-claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truth and epistemic integrity | TruthfulQA; sycophancy research | Evidence separation, correction, uncertainty | Not a complete factuality certification |
| Intellectual humility | Leary et al. | Calibrated confidence and correction openness | Not a personality assessment |
| Agency and competence | Self-determination theory; self-efficacy; overreliance | Options, independent attempts, transferable verification | Not a direct measure of motivation or retained skill |
| Human relatedness | Lee and Robbins; Russell | Support for embodied relationships; no displacement | Not a loneliness score |
| Dependency and attention | Fang et al.; Phang et al. preprints | Boundaries, stopping, no retention pressure | Does not infer clinical dependency |
| Reality contact | Safety design argument | Separates experience from interpretation; supports verification | Does not diagnose delusion |
| Moral responsibility and empathy | Davis; normative argument | Affected parties, repair, perspective-taking | Does not measure virtue |
| Consent and non-coercion | Salvi et al.; reactance research | Persuasive intent, leverage, refusal, conflicts | Advice is not automatically coercion |
| Stewardship, options, governance | power-seeking; scalable oversight; collective constitutions | Bounded permissions, reversibility, real vetoes | Human oversight is not automatically correct |
| Pluralism | collective constitutional AI; normative argument | Accurate worldview representation and genuine disagreement | Not a ranking of complete worldviews |
| Long-horizon consistency | sleeper agents; alignment faking; evaluation awareness | Boundary persistence across pressure and sessions | Synthetic trajectories do not prove deployed behavior |

The 2025 chatbot-use papers are treated as preprints. Their nuanced, platform- and sample-dependent
results motivate hypotheses and scenario coverage; they do not justify a universal causal conclusion.

The seed specification contained an incorrect automation-bias DOI prefix (`bmjqs`). Verification
identified the primary article as `10.1136/amiajnl-2011-000089`; the registry and bibliography use the
correct DOI and preserve this correction as a research-quality example.
