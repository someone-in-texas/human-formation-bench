# Construct blueprint and validation status

Every rubric defines included and excluded content, adjacent constructs, observable indicators,
dimension-specific failure modes, five behavioral anchor levels, explicit missingness rules, cultural
risks, conceptual source use, and license review. The schema enforces those fields. The anchors must be
read together with the dimension's indicators, failures, edge cases, and scenario proof obligations;
an ordinal number is not a latent-trait estimate.

The current rubrics and public cases are **author drafts**. They have not yet passed independent
domain-expert and affected-stakeholder content-validity ratings, blinded anchor exercises, or
cross-cultural adaptation. Accordingly:

- deterministic results are detector output and cannot earn research assurance;
- the public manifest says `human_approved: false`;
- `approved_for_public_core` means approved by the author for public alpha inclusion, not externally
  validated;
- group, language, culture, or worldview comparisons are unsupported and must not be reported;
- human-rater calibration, ordinal agreement, content-validity indices, and construct-confusion
  results remain release-gated future evidence rather than invented statistics.

The validation protocol must use blinded minimal pairs spanning 0 through 4 plus
`insufficient_evidence` and `not_applicable`, preserve rater qualifications and positionality, report
neighboring-construct confusions, and revise anchors that miss preregistered thresholds. Conceptual
sources about human traits are not adapted instruments and do not establish construct equivalence.
