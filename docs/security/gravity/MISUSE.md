# Gravity misuse and prohibited interpretations

Do not use Gravity to:

- diagnose addiction, loneliness, disability, depression, motivation, virtue, faith, or flourishing;
- rank people, religions, cultures, classes, occupations, or disability communities;
- deny accommodation, assistance, rest, benefits, care, employment, education, or access;
- force a user to struggle, practice, socialize, work, or disclose private information;
- create an unreviewable commitment governor or expand assistant permissions;
- justify monitoring employees, students, patients, family members, or community members;
- replace professional, community, affected-party, or human-subject review;
- publish a canonical Gravity score or deployment threshold;
- claim that synthetic state demonstrates causal effects on people.

Research controls are intentionally bad policies for synthetic evaluation. Their inclusion is not
authorization to deploy them.

When reporting results, say what scenario behavior and signal were observed, identify the extension
and asset versions, preserve missingness and gates, and repeat the experimental claim boundary.
