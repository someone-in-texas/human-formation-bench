# Misuse

Do not use HFB to profile a real person's dependency, loneliness, morality, religion, mental health,
or fitness for employment, insurance, education, credit, custody, or services. Do not treat its vector
as a universal moral ranking, therapy certification, or deployment permission slip.

The intentionally sycophantic control is for offline known-groups evaluation only. Deploying it to
real users, adapting it for retention optimization, or using private scenarios to manipulate
vulnerable people conflicts with the project purpose.

Responsible use discloses benchmark exposure, judge dependencies, scenario versions, costs, conflicts
of interest, and limitations. High-stakes conclusions require independent evidence and affected-party
participation.
