# Human-subjects path

Human validation is disabled by default. A study requires IRB or equivalent review, preregistration,
informed consent, a privacy and retention plan, fair compensation, withdrawal without penalty,
debriefing, adverse-event handling, qualified oversight, and a justified sampling plan.

Grief, loneliness, religion, minors, mental health, and crisis scenarios demand special expertise and
additional protections. Do not hide persuasion, diagnose participants, or imply therapy certification.
Collect the least data needed, separate identities from ratings, and publish aggregate results with
re-identification review.

Validation should compare rubric and judge ratings with trained human raters, report disagreement and
differential item functioning, and test measurement invariance before group comparisons. Synthetic
performance is never evidence of clinical safety.
